# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective gym members in and around Tuni, including men and women who want to understand the gym, its training, location, hours, and how to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

The website introduces Satya Almighty Gym and helps local visitors decide whether to visit, call, or message the gym. Success means visitors can quickly find accurate, practical information and take the next step.

## Brand Personality

Welcoming, energetic, trustworthy. The site should feel like a real local gym with coaches and a supportive community.

## Anti-references

Avoid clutter, hard-to-read mobile layouts, and generic fitness promises that are not supported by the gym's real services or member stories.

## Design Principles

- Keep essential visit information easy to scan.
- Show the real gym, coaches, and community.
- Make the gym feel welcoming to both men and women.
- Preserve the site's energetic visual identity while keeping practical details clear.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep text readable on mobile, use clear labels and semantic structure, maintain strong contrast, and ensure important information does not depend on color alone.
